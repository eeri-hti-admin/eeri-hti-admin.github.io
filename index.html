Who we are

From a campus team to an international chapter

Founded in May 2023 as the CornerStone team in the Civil Engineering department, we became the EERI HTI Student Chapter in October 2024, officially representing the Earthquake Engineering Research Institute at HTI and competing in the Seismic Design Competition.

Signature projects

Two towers, two stories

SDC 2025 · UC Berkeley

Tori Tower

A 19-story mixed-use tower inspired by Kobe’s Torii gate: commercial, cultural, office and hotel zones linked by a walkable spine, with a Kumiko-inspired double-skin façade. Balsa wood, designed to ASCE/SEI 7-22 Design Earthquake and MCER levels.

ETABSAutoCADRevitOutriggersKobe, Japan
Elevate & Shake 2024 · BUE

The Pearl Tower

A 160 cm, 19-floor balsa tower engineered for one-directional shaking: less material in the quiet axis, X-bracing, a belt system and vertical outriggers placed at the floors that carry the dead weights.
